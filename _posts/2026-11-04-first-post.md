Hey there! This is my first post, and I just wanted to give a brief intro about where I'm at and how this blog might go.

## Me, right now

I'm Ryan and I'm currently in the trenches of the third year of my PhD. I've just finished writing my third paper this year: I finished [KDFP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10854) in March, and then wrote [RAPID](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04081) and [Follow the Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04085) back to back in September and October. I've got a couple of different possible plans for the future, so I'll wait to elaborate on that until I see how things play out over the next few weeks.


## Blog info

I'm not really sure how often I'll be updating this blog. I don't have much of an online presence (for many, many reasons), but the idea of a personal blog seems the most palatable to me. I'm very much somebody who prefers interacting with people IRL; I even commute to USC just to meet with my advisor in person, instead of over a Zoom call. 

Should I ever feel like I have something worth saying on the internet, it'll end up being here. Whether it ends up being work-related, personal interests, a place to document my thoughts, or some combination of these, I'm not quite sure yet. I'm very much an open book if you talk to me in person, but the internet is an entirely different monster. We'll see how things go, but for now, thanks for visting!
